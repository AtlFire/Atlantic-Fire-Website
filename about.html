About us

Built by a wildland firefighter, for the homeowners in his own community.

Atlantic Wildfire Mitigation & Co helps homeowners, cottage owners and communities in Halifax and the surrounding area reduce wildfire risk around their properties.

Meet Daniel Bowers

I grew up in the woods.

In 2023, the wildfire in Tantallon hit my own community, in the subdivision where I live. Not long after, I started working for the provincial natural resources department and joined a wildland fire crew. Since then I've deployed to fires across the country.

Along the way I saw a gap. Homeowners hear that wildfire is a risk, but few have someone who will walk their property with them, explain what actually matters, and help them do something about it.

That's why I started Atlantic Wildfire Mitigation & Co: to give homeowners a clear plan they can keep, and to help carry it out.

  • Certified forestry and wildlife technician
  • Years of wildland firefighting experience, including deployments across Canada
  • Additional training and courses in fire and forestry

How we work

Practical, honest, and local.

1

We assess

Daniel walks your property with you, checks each zone around your home, and takes photos as we go.

2

You get a plan

A written plan with priorities, a yearly calendar and an emergency preparedness checklist.

3

We can do the work

If you want help, we quote the work in your plan in writing, and carry it out with our crew.

What we believe

No scare tactics.

  • Practical, not scary. We show you what you can do.
  • Honest about limits. We can't make a property fireproof, and we won't say we can.
  • Plans you keep. Something you can come back to every spring and fall.
  • Local. We work in the communities we live in.

An independent company

Atlantic Wildfire Mitigation & Co is an independent, privately owned company. We are not part of, or endorsed by, any government agency or program, including the provincial natural resources department and municipal fire services.

Some municipalities offer their own free assessments. We're not part of those programs. We offer our own written plans and work.
