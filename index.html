Why now

Wildfire is a Nova Scotia problem.

In 2023, wildfires burned in Shelburne County and in the Halifax area near Upper Tantallon. Since then, homeowners and insurers have been paying closer attention to what surrounds each property.

Most of what protects a home is close to the house: what's against the walls, under the deck, on the roof and in the gutters, and how close the trees are.

  • 1.5 m strip around the house and deck kept free of anything that can burn.
  • About 10 m is a common target distance between the house and spruce, fir, pine or cedar.
  • About 3 m between tree crowns makes it harder for fire to travel tree to tree.

What you get

A plan worth keeping.

Your report isn't a one-time checklist. It's a document you can come back to each spring and fall.

  • Plain-language results and your first three steps

  • A priority action list you can tick off

An honest promise

We can't make a property fireproof, and we won't tell you we can. We can help you reduce the things that let fire reach your home, and tell you what's worth doing first.

We also don't promise changes to your insurance. Ask your insurer what it looks for, and we'll give you a report you can share.

Know the rules

Fire season rules in Nova Scotia

Season
Wildfire risk season runs March 15 to October 15.
Daily burn check
Restrictions update at 2 p.m. daily. No brush burning or campfires from 8 a.m. to 2 p.m. during the season.
The fine
Breaking burn restrictions can carry a $25,000 fine.
Report a fire
Call 1-800-565-2224 or 911.

Check the current status at novascotia.ca/burnsafe. Rules can change, so always check the official source.

Questions

Good questions we hear.

Isn't there a free assessment?

Some municipalities, including Halifax, offer free FireSmart property assessments. That's a great starting point. If you'd like a written plan you keep, priced actions, and someone who can do the work, that's what we offer.

Will this lower my insurance?

We can't promise that. Insurers are paying closer attention to what surrounds a property, so ask yours what it looks for. We'll give you a dated report with photos that you can share.

Are you FireSmart certified?

Our assessments are based on general FireSmart concepts. We are not affiliated with FireSmart Canada unless stated in writing.

Where do you work?

Nova Scotia. Tell us your town in the request form and we'll confirm.

When do you work?

Mostly during the growing season. Work can pause on days when provincial burn restrictions or woods closures apply, and we reschedule at no charge.

Do I have to be home?

For the assessment, yes. It's the best way to walk the property together and answer your questions.
