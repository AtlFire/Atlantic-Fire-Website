Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Effective date: September 20, 2026

1. Who we are

Atlantic Wildfire Mitigation & Co ("we", "us") is a Nova Scotia company that provides wildfire risk assessments, written readiness plans, mitigation work and maintenance for homes, cottages and communities. Our legal name is Atlantic Wildfire Mitigation & Forestry Company. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and what we do with it.

2. Who is responsible for privacy

Daniel Bowers, Owner, is responsible for how we handle personal information. You can reach us at mitigation@atlanticwildfire.com or (902) 237-8909.

3. What information we collect

Depending on how you deal with us, we may collect:

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, email address, and town or address.
  • Property details: the address, property type, features of the home and grounds, and information you tell us about your property.
  • Photos and notes: photos and notes we take during a site visit, which are part of your assessment record.
  • Business records: quotes, invoices, payments and messages between us.
  • Website information: the companies that host our website and provide its fonts may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type when you visit. We do not currently use advertising or analytics cookies.

We do not ask for information we don't need. Please don't send sensitive information, like financial account details, through our contact form.

4. How our contact form works

Our request form opens your own email program with your message ready to send. Nothing is sent to us until you send that email. Your message and the details in it then come to us by email.

5. Why we collect it

We use personal information to:

  • reply to your questions and requests,
  • book and carry out visits, assessments and work,
  • prepare your written plan and property record,
  • track a property's progress over time and support future visits and maintenance,
  • quote, invoice and keep our business records,
  • meet legal, tax and insurance requirements, and
  • improve our services.

We ask for your consent before we collect, use or share your information. When you contact us, book a visit or agree to a quote, you agree that we may use your information for these purposes. You can withdraw consent at any time, though we may then be unable to provide a service.

We will only send you promotional messages if you have agreed to receive them, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

6. Property records

We keep a record of the properties we assess, including your plan, photos and notes. This lets us track a property's progress over time and helps us serve you again. We may use records in combined, anonymous form, for example a count of properties assessed. We will not use information that identifies you or your property for marketing, or share it with anyone else, without your permission.

7. Photos

Photos we take during a visit are used for your plan and our records. We will not publish photos of your property, home or family, or your testimonial, unless you give us written permission. If you agree, we will not show your address or other identifying details.

8. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information. We may share it with:

  • service providers who help us run our business, such as email, storage, software, accounting and payment providers,
  • subcontractors, such as an arborist, only when needed for work you have approved,
  • your insurer or another party, only when you ask us to,
  • authorities, if required by law.

Some of these providers may store information outside Nova Scotia or outside Canada.

9. How long we keep it

We keep client and property records for as long as we provide services to you, and for 7 years afterwards, unless you ask us to delete them sooner and we don't need them for legal or tax reasons. Marketing contact details are kept until you ask us to stop. After that we delete or anonymize the information.

10. How we protect it

We use reasonable safeguards, such as password protection and limited access, for the information we hold. No system is completely secure. If a breach creates a real risk of serious harm, we will tell affected people and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as required.

11. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct anything that is wrong, delete it where we no longer need it, and stop using it for certain purposes. To make a request, contact us using the details in section 2. We will reply within 30 days, and we may need to confirm who you are first.

12. Complaints

If you have a concern, please contact us first. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, which oversees the federal privacy law for businesses (PIPEDA).

13. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
